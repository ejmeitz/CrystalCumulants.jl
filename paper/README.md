This folder contains the final data used in the paper entitled "Quantum Anharmonic Phonon Thermodynamics from the Free Energy Cumulant Expansion" by Ethan Meitz, Alois Castellano, Gerald Wang and Alan McGaughey.

The `tar.gz` folders contain the force constants, supercell, unitcell, thermodynamic results (i.e. `F_mean.txt`), as well as the effect of sample size on the results.
